Deep Learning-Based Automated Medical Image Segmentation Using Attention U-Net Architecture

Smaranjit Ghose, Collaborator A, Collaborator B

Proceedings of IEEE International Conference on Computer Vision and Image Processing (ICCVIP 2023)

We propose a novel attention-based U-Net architecture for automated segmentation of medical images, specifically targeting MRI brain scans. The model leverages multi-scale attention gates to focus on relevant regions, achieving state-of-the-art Dice scores on benchmark datasets.

Multilingual Hate Speech Detection Using Transfer Learning and Cross-Lingual Transformers

Smaranjit Ghose, Collaborator C

Neural Computing and Applications, Springer

This paper presents a comprehensive study on multilingual hate speech detection leveraging XLM-RoBERTa and multilingual BERT. We evaluate the models on 6 languages and demonstrate strong cross-lingual transfer, achieving competitive F1 scores.

Real-Time Gesture Recognition for Sign Language Translation Using 3D Convolutional Networks

Smaranjit Ghose, Collaborator D, Collaborator E

IEEE International Conference on Multimedia and Expo (ICME 2022)

We present a real-time sign language recognition system using 3D convolutional neural networks and temporal attention mechanisms. The system achieves 91.2% accuracy on the WLASL dataset and runs at 30 FPS on standard hardware.
